# Synthesis — What Makes a Generated World *Feel* Real

Companion to `SYNTHESIS.md` (which distills the *technical* corpus,
dockets 01-11). This distills the craft corpus — dockets 12-15: literary
worldbuilding theory, the feel doctrines of shipped world-simulators,
toponymy-as-archaeology, and tellability/prose craft. Where `SYNTHESIS.md`
answers *"is the world right?"*, this answers *"does the world land?"*

## The five feel-laws

**1. Perfect consistency beneath, deliberate incompleteness above.**
(Tolkien, Sanderson, Harrison, Qud — 12, 13.) The engine must never
violate its own laws; the telling must never exhaust them. Depth is
implied consistency: small surface gestures whose submerged mass is
*actually computed*, so every inference a reader makes is true. And the
strongest single trick is the withheld fragment — the Berúthiel emission,
the unexplained ruin name, the disputed account. The sim is the iceberg;
the chronicle shows the tip and *guarantees* the rest.

**2. Time must leave residue.** (Lived-in essays, DF, toponymy — 12, 14.)
Growth alone reads as spotless stasis. Real places accumulate wear: ruins
and abandoned roads, settlements that outlive their founding reason,
river names older than the nations on their banks, old names worn opaque
by phonological erosion while colonies stay transparent. A world feels
old exactly insofar as its map is a palimpsest.

**3. Coherence through citation, not decoration.** (URR, Le Guin, KoDP —
12, 13.) One cultural seed read by every subsystem — names, laws, omens,
architecture, market quirks — so unrelated surfaces visibly agree. Myth
must be load-bearing (mechanical stakes), names must be generated by the
culture's phonology, and constraints/taboos define a culture more than
capabilities. Randomness reads as authored symbolism when it is
thematically seeded.

**4. Story is sifted and mythologized, never logged.** (Qud FDG, Ryan,
Sylvester, narratology — 13, 15, cross-ref 05.) Ground truth stays in the
state for the harness; the telling compresses and distorts it into legend.
Selection is scored (eventfulness = norm-violation × stakes × reversal),
pacing has valleys, and enough causal proximity is surfaced that
apophenia — the reader connecting the dots — does the final narration
for free.

**5. Specificity must have consequence, and memory must accrete.**
(Short's oatmeal law, DF artifacts, Wildermyth — 13, 15.) A detail that
never matters later teaches the reader to skip details. Named artifacts
with custody chains, epithets earned from deeds, callbacks on second
mention, scars rendered on the map itself — the anchor is always a
*thing that persists visibly*, never a menu entry.

## Where Calliope stands

Law 1: engine half honored (deterministic, causally closed); telling half
absent — the chronicle explains everything and withholds nothing.
Law 2: **the largest gap** — nothing in the world can currently die,
decay, or be abandoned; the map has no memory of its own history.
Law 3: culture flavors names only; myth is decorative; constraints exist
in the sim but are never surfaced as culture.
Law 4: machinery gap already tracked (M6); the *scoring/mythologizing*
stance from 13/15 refines what M6.4-6.5 should build.
Law 5: entities are re-introduced identically forever; no artifact
biographies; no consequential-detail discipline.

## The one-sentence version

Calliope already *computes* a true world; it does not yet *remember* one —
the feel program is: let things die and leave marks (residue), let names
carry time (strata), let the telling select, mythologize and withhold
(tellability), and let every specific detail either return or belong to
the atmosphere on purpose.
